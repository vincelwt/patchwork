Patchwork

Privacy policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Patchwork is an open-source workspace that connects people with AI agents. This policy describes the Patchwork mobile app, patchwork.sh, and the optional managed relay. A self-hosted workspace is also governed by the policies of the person or organization operating it.

Data Patchwork handles

  • Workspace identity, such as your display name, optional email address, avatar, role, and device label.
  • Content you and your teammates provide, including messages, tasks, questions, files, links, reactions, agent runs, and automation history.
  • Connection and security data needed to pair devices, authenticate requests, keep the workspace synchronized, and prevent abuse.
  • Photos you choose to attach and speech you choose to dictate.

How data is used

Patchwork uses this data only to provide, secure, synchronize, and support the workspace. The mobile app has no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK and Patchwork does not sell personal data.

AI providers and other processors

Workspace administrators choose which agents and model providers run in their workspace. When an agent is triggered, your message and relevant recent workspace context may be sent to that configured provider. Providers can include third parties such as OpenAI or Anthropic, or a model running on hardware controlled by the workspace operator. Ask your workspace administrator which providers are configured before sharing content.

Self-hosted workspaces send data to the relay and providers selected by their operator. When a workspace uses the managed relay, encrypted app traffic is routed through Cloudflare and Patchwork's managed relay service to the workspace host. If you use dictation, iOS may send audio to Apple for speech recognition when on-device recognition is unavailable. Apple handles that audio under its own privacy policy.

On-device storage and permissions

The mobile access token is stored in device-only secure storage. Recent workspace data and drafts are cached on the device for offline access and are isolated to that credential. Signing out removes the token, workspace cache, and cached images from the device. Camera access is used only to scan a pairing code. Photo access is used only when you select an attachment, and microphone and speech access are used only when you start dictation.

Retention, access, and deletion

The workspace operator controls server retention and backups. Revoking a device removes its access but does not erase workspace history. Removing a member deactivates access while retaining messages, tasks, and other shared records so conversations remain understandable. Contact your workspace administrator to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your data. The operator may retain or de-identify shared records where deletion would affect other members or where retention is required.

Security

Patchwork uses HTTPS or WSS for remote connections, short-lived one-use pairing secrets, hashed credentials at rest on the relay, and a separate revocable token for each mobile device. No system can guarantee absolute security, so workspace operators should keep the relay and agent hosts updated and restrict membership.

Children

Patchwork is a work tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Questions and changes

For privacy or security questions, contact your workspace administrator or the Patchwork maintainers through GitHub Security. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
